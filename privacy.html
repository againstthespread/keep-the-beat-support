Privacy
Policy.

Keep the Beat is a rhythm game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, what it doesn’t send, and what happens if you choose to contact support.

Effective date November 3, 2026

01

Game data stays in the app

The current Keep the Beat iOS implementation stores game information in the app’s local storage on your device. This includes your selected difficulty, sound and haptics settings, unlocked difficulty levels, run totals, personal bests, and a summary of your most recent run.

A run summary can include its difficulty, tempo, streak, result, a timing summary, and completion date. This information is used to show your progress and keep your preferences between sessions. It is not associated with a profile or game account.

02

Personal information

The app does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other profile information to play. The game data described above is stored locally and is not sent to the developer during normal play.

Keep the Beat does not provide cloud sync or online multiplayer.

03

Sharing and network activity

The iOS project reviewed for this policy contains no app network requests or remote service integrations for game data. The app does not intentionally share your records with the developer or other companies. iOS may include app data in device backups according to the backup settings you choose; those backups are handled by Apple.

04

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

The version described here does not include advertising, external analytics, or tracking code. Its privacy manifest declares that it does not track users and lists no collected data types. The app has no advertising SDK or analytics SDK.

05

Retention and deletion

Game data remains in the app’s local storage while it is installed. There is currently no in-app control to clear records. To remove the local copy, delete Keep the Beat from your iPhone; removing only its Home Screen icon does not delete the app. A copy in an iOS device backup may remain under your backup settings and could return if you restore that backup.

The app does not maintain a server account or receive a copy of your game records, so there are no remote game records for the developer to delete.

06

If you email support

Contacting support is voluntary. If you send an email, the information you choose to include is sent through your email provider and received by the support contact below. We use it to respond to your question. Email providers may store the message under their own policies and your account settings.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to respond to and manage your request, unless we must retain it longer for legal or security reasons. You can ask us to delete a support message by emailing the same address. Please include only information needed to describe the issue.

07

This support website

This is a static website. It has no contact form, advertising, analytics, or cookie-setting code. GitHub Pages serves the pages and may handle information about website requests under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

08

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them. The effective date at the top shows when the current version was published.
