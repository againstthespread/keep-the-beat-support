04 / NEED A HAND?

A few quick
fixes.

If something still feels off, send a note and we’ll help you get back in time.

Why can’t I hear the beat?

Check that Sound is on in the game settings, then check your iPhone’s volume and Silent Mode. The game respects Silent Mode. If sound still won’t start, close and reopen the game.

My taps feel late or inconsistent. What should I try?

Wireless audio can add delay. For the most consistent feel, try the built-in speaker or wired audio, then start a fresh run and follow the count-in. The game judges each tap against the beat; it does not move the rhythm to follow your taps.

How do I unlock the next difficulty?

Reach 10 successful consecutive beats on the difficulty you have unlocked. The next level opens automatically and remains unlocked on this device.

My progress or the game doesn’t look right.

Close and reopen Keep the Beat, then try one new run. If the issue continues, email support with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Please leave out anything personal.
